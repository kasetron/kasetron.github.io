title: "Kasetron Modular"
---
Build log of my DIY modular synthesizer, made mostly from salvaged parts.

**Almost every module here is built from designs published by [Eddy Bergman](https://www.eddybergman.com/)**; all credit for the circuits goes to him. Each page links the original article and only records what is useful to someone building the same thing: parts, gotchas and my modifications. The exceptions are the **output module** (my own design) and a few **modifications to the VCO**.
